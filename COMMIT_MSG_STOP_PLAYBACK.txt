stopAudioPlayback -> stopPlayback: one teardown for both lanes

The method was never audio-specific. Its body stops the
process-global mpv engine and sends MediaPlaybackService
ACTION_STOP - neither of which knows or cares which lane loaded
the media.

It became a lie the moment video needed it. exitPipAndFinish()
only finishes PlayerActivity and tears the SurfaceView down; the
engine outlives the Activity. So closing a video closed the window
while the media carried on - audio still audible, notification
still posted, engine still advancing - with no window left to stop
it. Reported as "closing doesn't actually close".

The name was part of the cause: an app reaching for a teardown
found only an *audio* teardown, so wiring video to it looked wrong
and was deferred.

Also adds commands.stopPlayback() so consumers call one facade
instead of reaching through getMpvPlayerModule(). stop() keeps its
meaning - halt the engine, keep the session and notification
available - because that is correct for "pause the media, keep the
transport", and wrong for "close".

Breaking: stopAudioPlayback is removed rather than aliased. An
alias would leave a name that lies about the operation; a consumer
reaching for it would get undefined and skip the teardown silently.
The app is the only consumer and moves in the same change.
