V13 Phase 54 DX: `useLaunchParams()` hook for consumer
App.tsx branches that render the module's <PlayerRoot /> when
the activity is launched with a queued playback payload, vs the
regular navigator when no params.

The hook reads the one-shot launch-params queue from the bridge
exactly once on mount. Subsequent reads return null. Returns
LaunchParams | null. Exported from the module index.

Junior-dev rule of thumb: if your app routes to <PlayerRoot />,
call this hook and check the return. No need to manage the
queue, native one-shot, or React state yourself.
